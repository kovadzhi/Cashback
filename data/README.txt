Verified partner-building candidate pool, 2026-11-05.
2344 distinct addressed Moscow buildings spread across the city.

Address tags: https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/ways
Roof polygons and address-label coordinates: OpenFreeMap planet 20261101,
OpenMapTiles building and housenumber layers, zoom 14.
Each housenumber label's OSM way ID and number were checked against
that way's address tags. Coordinates pass point-in-roof checks including
courtyard-hole exclusion. Substations and non-house auxiliary structures
were excluded. These are prototype locations, not verified brand branches.
